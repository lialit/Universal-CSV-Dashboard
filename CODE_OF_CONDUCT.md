# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Universal CSV Dashboard a welcoming,
respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that supports a positive community include:

- showing empathy and respect;
- giving constructive, specific feedback;
- focusing discussion on the work rather than the person;
- accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes;
- respecting privacy, confidentiality and security boundaries;
- helping newcomers understand the project and contribution process.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, intimidation, discrimination or personal attacks;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- sharing confidential datasets, credentials, exports or security details;
- sustained disruption of project discussions;
- sexualized language, imagery or unwanted attention;
- retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including Issues, Pull
Requests, Discussions, release notes, review comments and other public or private
project communication. It also applies when someone represents the project in a
public space.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.
They may edit or remove comments, commits, code, Issues and other contributions
that violate this Code of Conduct. They may also issue warnings, temporarily
restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub.
Do not post sensitive personal details in a public Issue.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible. Information will be
shared only with people who need it to evaluate and resolve the situation.

## Enforcement guidelines

Possible responses include:

1. **Correction** — a private explanation and request to correct behavior.
2. **Warning** — a formal warning with clear consequences for repetition.
3. **Temporary restriction** — a time-limited restriction from project spaces.
4. **Permanent ban** — removal from all project participation.
