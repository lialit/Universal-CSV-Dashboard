# Universal CSV Dashboard

## Product Vision

Save people hours of manual analysis by automatically transforming CSV files into clear business insights.

## Target Audience

- Small businesses
- Consultants
- Freelancers
- Analysts
- Marketing teams
- E-commerce companies

## Core Promise

Upload CSV.

Understand your business.

Make better decisions.

## Positioning

A time-saving analytics assistant with a user-friendly experience.

## Product Principles

- Automation First
- Insights Before Charts
- Business Language
- Beautiful by Default
- Privacy First
- Modern Engineering

## Success Metric

A new user should understand an unfamiliar dataset in under 60 seconds.

## North Star

Every feature must save users time.
