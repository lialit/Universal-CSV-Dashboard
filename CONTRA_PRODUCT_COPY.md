## Product description
Build a polished data dashboard without starting from scratch.

This reusable Streamlit template lets you upload a CSV file, map your date, numeric and category columns, and automatically generate a modern analytics application.

- Common encoding and delimiter support
- Configurable primary metric and categories
- Executive KPI cards
- Interactive Plotly charts
- Time-series and category analysis
- Distribution and correlation views
- Missing-value and duplicate-row reporting
- Cleaned CSV download
- Responsive multipage design
- Modern Streamlit APIs
- Sample dataset and setup guide
