## Core direction

> **Help people understand business data in minutes instead of hours.**

Universal CSV Dashboard is building toward the easiest trusted starting point
for understanding structured business data.

The product should:

- reduce repetitive analytical setup;
- keep important assumptions visible and editable;
- connect observations to evidence;
- make data quality part of every conclusion;
- preserve a complete local-first workflow;
- help users reach better questions without replacing their judgment.

## Core promise

**Upload data. Understand it. Decide what comes next.**

For the full mission, long-term vision and product principles, use the
canonical documents linked above. This file remains as a stable entry point for
the numbered documentation structure and should not contain a separate version
